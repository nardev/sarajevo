  <h2 style="font-family: var(--font-display); font-size: 38px; font-weight: 600; margin: 0 0 12px">About this project</h2>
  <p style="color: var(--text-dim); max-width: 60ch; font-size: 16px; line-height: 1.7">
    Sarajevo in Songs is a small curated catalogue of songs that mention
    the city across genres, languages and decades. Each entry collects
    the basics (artist, year, album), a short note on the Sarajevo
    reference, an excerpt of the lyrics, and links out to the usual
    places: YouTube, Discogs, Wikipedia and Genius.
  </p>
  <p style="color: var(--text-dim); max-width: 60ch; font-size: 16px; line-height: 1.7">
    Built as a static Jekyll site. To suggest a song, open an issue or
    pull request against <code>_data/songs.yml</code>.
  </p>
</section>
